Privacy Policy

Jesus Believers Association Church (JBAC) • JBAC-AP Android Mobile Application & Web Platform

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Jesus Believers Association Church (JBAC) (“We”, “Us”, or “Our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our members, pastors, students, counselors, and community users. This Privacy Policy governs the JBAC-AP Mobile Application and the official website https://jbac.in (collectively, the “Platform”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information that you provide voluntarily when registering, updating your profile, or utilizing our community directory services:

  • Personal & Identity Details: Full name, gender, date of birth, educational background, and community category (Believer, Pastor, Student, Church, Pastors Association, Doctor / Counselor).
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number and optional email address. These are used for authentication (login and OTP verification), communication, and customer support.
  • Geographic Location Details: State (Andhra Pradesh), District, Assembly Constituency, Mandal / Municipality, and Village / Ward name. This information is submitted by you to connect members with local church directories, community representatives, and pastors.
  • Optional User Uploads: Profile photos, church premises photos, or documents uploaded explicitly by you.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve our spiritual, educational, and community welfare services:

  • To verify your identity and manage secure login access to your account.
  • To enable directory searches for local churches, pastors, marriage counseling, and prayer support.
  • To communicate essential updates, prayer notifications, and community welfare programs.
  • To comply with statutory and legal obligations in India.

3. Mobile Device Permissions (JBAC-AP Android App)

The JBAC-AP mobile app requests only the minimum essential permissions necessary for functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to retrieve directory listings, news, and services from our secure servers.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional): Requested only when you choose to select a profile photo or church picture to upload. We do not inspect, collect, or store any other files or media on your device.
  • NO Background Tracking: We do NOT access your background GPS location, contacts, calendar, SMS, camera, or microphone.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We strictly DO NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data to third parties, advertising brokers, or marketing agencies.

Your information is only shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent (such as displaying pastor contact details in the public directory for churchgoers seeking spiritual support).
  • With vetted cloud hosting infrastructure providers (AWS Amazon Web Services) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection requirements.
  • When required by law, court order, or official governmental directive.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical measures to safeguard your data. All communications between the app and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Database assets are stored securely on AWS RDS MySQL with KMS encryption at rest.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion Policy

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Update Information: You can edit and update your personal details at any time by logging into the app or website.
  • Account & Data Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, you may submit a request to support@jbac.in or call +91 7075482182. All personal data will be completely purged from our active databases within 7 working days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are aimed at general community and family members. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The latest version will always be published directly at https://jbac.in/privacypolicy.
