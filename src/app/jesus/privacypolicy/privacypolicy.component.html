Privacy Policy - JBAC


Privacy Policy for JBAC-AP Mobile App and Web Platform

Last Updated: October 2026

Jesus Believers Association Church (JBAC) (“We”, “Us”, or “Our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the JBAC-AP Mobile Application and the official website https://jbac.in (collectively referred to as the “Platform”).


1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when registering, creating a profile, or using community services on the Platform. This includes:

  • Account & Profile Details: Full name, gender, date of birth, educational background, and community category (Believer, Pastor, Student, Church, Pastors Association, Doctor/Counselor).
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number and optional email address (used for OTP verification, account login, and service notifications).
  • Location Information: State (Andhra Pradesh), District, Assembly Constituency, Mandal / Municipality, and Village / Ward name (used to match members with local church directories, pastors, and community initiatives).
  • Optional User Content: Profile photos, church photographs, or certificates uploaded willingly by the user.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate community, spiritual, and organizational purposes:

  • To authenticate user sign-in and secure account access across the web and mobile app.
  • To facilitate church directory searches, prayer requests, matrimonial connections, and family counseling appointments.
  • To communicate official community announcements, welfare opportunities, and pastor fellowship meetings.
  • To maintain internal records required under applicable laws and regulations.

3. Device Permissions (Android Mobile App)

The JBAC-AP mobile app requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its features:

  • INTERNET & NETWORK_STATE: Required to communicate with our secure backend APIs and display up-to-date community information.
  • PHOTO / MEDIA / STORAGE ACCESS: Requested only when you explicitly choose to upload a profile picture, church photo, or event banner. We do not access, scan, or read any other files on your device.
  • NO Background Tracking: We do NOT access GPS in the background, read your contacts, record audio, or monitor device activity.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties, marketing firms, or advertising networks under any circumstances.

Information is shared only:

  • With your explicit consent (e.g. displaying registered pastors or churches in the public directory for members seeking spiritual guidance).
  • With trusted cloud hosting and database infrastructure providers (such as AWS Cloud) strictly bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • If mandated by lawful court orders, statutory regulations, or government authorities.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ modern administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between the mobile app, website, and our servers is encrypted using Industry-Standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Database records are stored in secure AWS RDS instances with KMS encryption at rest.

6. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

You retain full control over your personal data:

  • Review & Update: You may update your profile details at any time by logging into the app or website.
  • Account & Data Deletion Request: If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data permanently, you may send a deletion request to support@jbac.in or contact us via phone at +91 7075482182. Your request will be verified and completed within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for general community use and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 without parental or guardian consent.


8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The revised policy will always be posted directly at this URL (https://jbac.in/privacypolicy) with an updated effective date. Continued use of the platform after changes indicates your acceptance.
